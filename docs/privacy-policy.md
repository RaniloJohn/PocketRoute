# PocketRoute privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

PocketRoute is an offline network simulator. It does not collect, store
on a server, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The app has no user accounts.
- **No network access.** The simulation runs entirely on your device. The
  app does not send data over the internet.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no analytics,
  advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
- **Your lab files stay on your device.** Labs you save are stored in the
  app's own storage. They leave the device only when you choose
  **File › Share…**, which hands the file to the app you pick (email, Drive,
  chat and so on); that app's own privacy policy then applies.
- **Children.** The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
